Vendor onboarding gate and the weekly operating brief

A fictional office-services vendor can only be onboarded when its signed agreement, approved cost in GBP, accountable owner, current approvals and critical checklist all hold. The same workspace tracks milestones, late actions and owner follow-through, and issues a versioned weekly brief with explicit denominators.
