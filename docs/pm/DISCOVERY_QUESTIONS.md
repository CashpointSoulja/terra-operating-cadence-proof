# Discovery questions

These are the questions I would want answered in my first two weeks. I have no answers to any of them; the build uses synthetic assumptions in their place, and each assumption is named.

## Vendor onboarding

1. Who can approve office spend today, at what thresholds, and is that written down anywhere? *(Build assumes: budget owner always; finance lead above £25,000 a year or when cost is unknown.)*
2. Which onboarding steps are genuinely blocking versus nice-to-have? Who decided? *(Build assumes four critical steps: countersigned agreement, insurance certificate, data terms, bank call-back.)*
3. Is GBP the only currency office vendors are approved in, or are some contracted in EUR/USD? *(Build blocks non-GBP.)*
4. How long should an approval stay valid if nothing about the vendor changes? *(Build assumes 90 days.)*
5. What counts as a material change: price, scope, owner, the agreement version, the legal entity? Anything else (payment terms, notice period)?
6. Where do signed agreements live today, and who is the signatory of record?
7. Is there an existing vendor register, and how many active office vendors are there?
8. Who verifies bank details, and how? *(Build only records that a simulated call-back happened.)*

## Weekly cadence

9. What does the leadership meeting currently look at, and who prepares it?
10. Which KPIs do founders actually decide on each week, and which are reported out of habit?
11. When an action slips, what happens? Is it re-dated, reassigned, or silently dropped?
12. Should a late close count as a miss? *(Build says yes.)*
13. How many actions does an owner need before a follow-through rate is fair to show? *(Build says 3.)*
14. Who receives board materials, how far in advance, and in what format?
15. What must never appear in a board pack (personal data, unsigned commercials)?

## Constraints

16. Which tools are already paid for (finance, HRIS, document storage), so v2 integrates rather than adds?
17. Who would own this workflow if I were away for a week?
