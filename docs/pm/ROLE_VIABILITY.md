# Role viability (my own assessment)

## What I know

- A signed-out LinkedIn page lists **Business Operations Lead, Terra Industries, Greater London**, full-time, mid-senior, with duties across finance, HR, legal, vendor management, board materials, KPI tracking and leadership support.
- Terra's own careers page, when I checked, did not list this role. I have not found an ATS listing.
- Posting age is disputed: LinkedIn showed "2 weeks ago", RevPath one week, Eurojobs 17 September 2026. I do not treat the role as fresh or confirmed open.
- Salary is not published. My target of £60k and floor of £50k are my own numbers and are unverified against this role.

## Where this build is evidence of fit

| Posting duty | What the build shows |
|---|---|
| Vendor management, external service providers | Evidence-gated onboarding with approvals bound to the exact record |
| Finance | Integer-pence costs, annualisation, finance threshold, GBP-only policy |
| Legal (operational, not advice) | Agreement reference, version, signature and expiry checks; no legal advice given |
| Board materials | Board export only for an issued, current brief; audit trail |
| KPI tracking | Explicit numerators, denominators, minimum samples and unknowns |
| Leadership support, cross-functional projects | Milestones, actions, owner follow-through and decision requests |

## Gaps I would need to close

- No direct experience of Terra's sector, customers or regulatory context; this build stays deliberately civilian and administrative.
- UK employment, payroll and contract law sit with specialists; the tool tracks evidence, it does not judge it.
- I have not met the team or seen any internal process. Everything here is a starting proposal to be replaced by what discovery finds.

## Decision rule

I would only spend further time if the role is confirmed open through a Terra-controlled channel and the compensation range meets my floor.
